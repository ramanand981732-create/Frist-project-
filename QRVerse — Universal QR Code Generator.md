## FAQ

Static vs dynamic QR codes?

Static codes hold the content itself and can never change once printed. Dynamic codes hold a redirect link so the destination can be edited later; that needs a server and isn’t part of this version.

Can a QR code install an app?

No. It opens a store or download page; the person must finish installing.

Can I make a QR code for a file?

QR codes store text, not large files. Host the file (Drive, Dropbox, your server) and encode its share link. Files you pick here are never uploaded.

Why won’t my QR scan?

Use strong contrast with a dark foreground on a light background, keep the margin at 2+ and keep logos small with high error correction.
